Delete your Nele account

Last updated 18 November 2026

You can delete your Nele account and the data attached to it at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Nele app and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Sign in if you are not already.
  3. Tap Delete my account at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Confirm. Deletion happens immediately.

Option 2 — without the app

Email us from the address on your account, or just tell us the phone number or email you signed up with. We verify the request and delete the account within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

Send to support@nele.app with the subject "Delete my Nele account".

What gets deleted

  • Your account record: name, email and password hash.
  • Every listing you published, including its photos and documents.
  • All your chat conversations and messages.
  • Your saved properties and booking-agent requests.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Listings are also removed from public view immediately.

What may be kept

Short-lived server logs (for security and abuse investigation) age out on their normal retention window. We may retain a minimal record of a deletion request to be able to prove we honoured it.

Just want a break?

You do not have to delete your account to stop using Nele. You can mark properties as sold or delete individual listings, photos and documents from the app.
