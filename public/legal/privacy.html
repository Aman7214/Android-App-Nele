Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Nele is a property listing app for Raichur district. This page explains what the app collects, why, and how to get it deleted. We do not run advertising, we do not include third-party tracking SDKs, and we never sell personal data.

What we collect

  • Account: name, email, and a password stored only as a hash. Optional profile image.
  • Listings: title, property type, town, locality, price, area, bedrooms, bathrooms, and any photos or documents you attach.
  • Chat: messages you send in the app, with their timestamp and sender.
  • Agent requests: name, phone number, town, what you need, budget text and preferred call time.
  • Technical: server request logs used for debugging and abuse prevention.

What we do not collect

No advertising identifiers, no analytics or attribution SDKs, no contact-book access, no background location. Location is used only while choosing your district, and you can pick your town manually instead.

Who can see what

  • Public: listing details, listing photos, your display name, and whether a listing is active or sold.
  • Only you: your documents, email, saved properties and agent requests.
  • Both participants: messages in a conversation you are part of. Nobody else can read it.
  • Your phone number is never shown to other users.

Where data is stored

Nele runs on Neon (PostgreSQL, authentication, object storage and serverless functions) in the us-east-2 region, acting as our data processor. Photos sit in a public bucket so listings load fast. Documents and chat sit in private storage and are served only through short-lived signed links.

How long we keep it

Listings, photos, documents, chat and agent requests stay while your account exists. Deleting your account removes the account, listings, photos, documents, conversations, messages, saved properties and agent requests. Server logs age out on a short retention window.

Your choices

Edit or delete listings, individual photos and documents at any time. Delete the whole account from Profile → Delete account in the app, or follow the steps on the delete account page. You can also email support@nele.app and we will action your request within 30 days.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Authentication tokens are signed and verified on every request, and access to a listing, document or conversation is checked against your account on the server.

Children

Nele is for adults doing property business and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@nele.app
